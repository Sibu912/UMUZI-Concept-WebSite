>_ At Umuzi, no two learning journeys are the same. We partner with a range of organisations – local and global – to offer diverse, high-quality programmes designed to help you grow, earn, and thrive in the right industry.

Umuzi creates pathways to livelihood

Our programmes are diverse, built to meet different needs, career paths, and levels of experience.

{ }

Funded Programmes

These programmes are fully sponsored by our partners and are free to accepted learners.

Self-Funded Programmes

These opportunities allow learners to invest in themselves—often with flexible options like income-sharing agreements.

*These are just a few examples of the types of programmes we've run in the past, and continue to run with selected partners. It is possible that you are selected for a programme type not listed here, as our offerings evolve with industry changes.

*The availability of these programmes depends entirely on our partners and funding cycles. That's why not everyone is invited into a programme immediately after applying - even if you pass the aptitude test.

What it means to join the Umuzi community

When you complete your application and pass the aptitude test, you're not just waiting to get into a programme - you've already joined something powerful.

You become part of Umuzi's growing, global community of future talent, connected by a shared drive to learn, lead, and create change.

And while we work behind the scenes to match you with the right opportunity, you don't have to wait to start growing. Every learner in our community gains access to curated, high-value upskilling content to keep building momentum.
