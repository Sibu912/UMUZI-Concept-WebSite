STRATEGIC GROWTH SECTOR

We're building the next generation of coders, designers, and data talent to power Africa's digital economy. Through immersive training programmes, industry partnerships, and placement opportunities, Umuzi equips young people with front-end and back-end web development, data science, data engineering, UX/UI design, and project management skills.

We take a demand-led approach, working closely with enterprise partners to align training with real market needs. By connecting young people with the right skills to in-demand digital roles, experience, and support, Umuzi bridges the gap between potential and opportunity, helping young people move from learning to earning while meeting the talent needs of the digital economy.
