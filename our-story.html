From a photo club to a global community.

Umuzi was founded in 2008 by three friends from corporate South Africa, wanting to create change beyond boardrooms. In 2009, they started the Umuzi Photo Club, using photography to amplify young voices in informal settlements.

From art to industry

In these early years, we learned something important: while creative expression was powerful, photography alone wasn't leading to sustainable livelihoods.
2009 - 2014
Photo Club hosted events including
  • 32 workshops in 6 townships across 3 provinces
  • 30 local + 5 international exhibitions
  • Over 100 young creatives reached
2014
First Accredited Qualification
  • Launched our first accredited programme in graphic design and photography with 50 young creatives
  • Investec came on board as our first founding employer partner
2015
Expanding Creative Pathways
  • Introduced a copywriting qualification
  • Built a Creative Industry talent pipeline
2017
From Art to Tech
  • Added Design Thinking and Agile to the curriculum
  • Expanded into the Tech Industry with coding and web development programmes
2019
Scaling Skills
  • Launched data science and fast-track coding programmes.
2020
Radical Adaptation
  • COVID disrupted in-person learning
  • Shifted fully to remote learning, creating a bridge to a global future
2021-2022
Growing Partnerships
  • Scaled South African operations
  • Began global partnerships, including UNICEF
2023-2024
Global Impact
  • Launched programmes with global partners, including SAP
  • Expanded reach across continents and deepened global impact
2025-2028
Pathways for a Changing World
  • Expanded beyond digital to offer high-impact pathways in green innovation, tourism, agriculture, and care
  • Consolidated our methodology into three integrated Labs: Skills, Experience, and Launch strengthening learner journeys at every entry point
  • Deepened commitment to building future-fit talent pipelines that respond to emerging global needs
