SYS.CORE // CULTURE

On culture

We care deeply - about our people, our community, and our work. We're serious about impact, but we don't take ourselves too seriously. At Umuzi, you'll find a team that's smart, intentional, and committed to doing meaningful work in a way that's joyful, just, and human.

We Cultivate Joy

Work is more than a paycheck - it should bring meaning and energy to your life. At Umuzi, we celebrate wins big and small, face challenges with optimism, and create space for support, laughter, and kindness. Joy is shared; everyone helps make this a place where people feel valued and inspired. We bring curiosity, appreciation, and possibility into every interaction - because when we enjoy the journey together, we do our best work. Recognition isn't reserved for awards; your growth, ideas, and contributions are celebrated every day.

SYS.ENV // WORKSPACE

Remote, with intention

Remote work gives us flexibility, balance, and access to top talent aligned with our global mission. But we don't leave connection to chance. We've designed intentional ways to collaborate, connect, and keep the vibe strong.

In-person matters

We allocate a budget for in-person meetups and connection time throughout the year - whether it's coworking in Johannesburg or a brainstorm in a coastal café. Our annual in-person event, UWeek, brings the whole team together to align, connect, and celebrate.

Expand

We grow people

Our internal leadership development programme helps team members build the confidence, skills, and support to lead from wherever they are.

Who we hire
