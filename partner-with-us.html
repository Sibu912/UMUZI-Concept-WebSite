Invest in social capital at scale

>_ From future-ready talent pipelines to scalable learning systems, our partnerships bridge social impact and strategic outcomes. Work with us to enable human potential while meeting the evolving needs of your organisation.

Umuzi is a global community building pathways to livelihoods. We work with leading employers, governments, and development partners to design scalable, demand-driven workforce solutions that connect emerging talent to real economic opportunity.

Through our Skills, Experience, and Launch Labs, we design and deliver world-class learning journeys creating full-stack employability and sustainable livelihoods.

Whether you're funding impact, building inclusive talent pipelines, or shaping future-fit industries, Umuzi brings deep expertise, data-driven insight, and a strong track record of delivery.

How we partner and the models behind it

Design & Build A Custom Programme

We work closely with you to co-create a bespoke learning-to-earning pathway - from curriculum development and learner recruitment to delivery, mentorship, and placement. This model is ideal for partners who want to directly shape the future of their workforce or support system-level change.

Fund An Active Programme

Join us in scaling programmes that are already showing strong outcomes. Whether aligned to youth employment, gender equity, digital skills, or the green economy - we'll connect your funding to high-potential cohorts and provide rigorous impact reporting throughout.

Whether you're a philanthropic donor or a strategic funder, you can help:

  • Launch a full cohort aligned to specific sectors or regions
  • Sponsor one or many learners across a pathway
  • Back key components like data access, mentorship, or learner wellness
  • Plug into existing projects already delivering results

Whatever your size, strategy, or sector, we'll co-create a solution that delivers shared value for you and for the communities we serve.

Host Experience Opportunities

Through our Experience Lab, you can host project-based work, micro-internships, or short-term gigs for learners. This provides learners with essential real-world exposure - and gives your organisation access to skilled, supported talent on flexible terms.

Hire Resources From Our Talent Network

We maintain a curated pool of job-ready professionals trained in high-demand skills. Our Launch Lab offers talent matchmaking, interview support, and employer-readiness tools to ensure smooth integration and lasting impact.

Mentor A Learner Or Team

Join our mentorship network and contribute your expertise to the next generation of changemakers. This model is ideal for individuals or teams looking to share knowledge while deepening their organisation's commitment to inclusive development.

Just 1 mentor, 2 conversations, 3 meaningful actions - that's all it takes to help build confidence, social capital, and access to opportunity.

Each partnership is a chance to do something meaningful, measurable, and transformative.
