SYS.MOD // 01

Skills Lab: build your foundation

Whether you're just starting out in your career or switching directions, our Skills Lab is where your journey begins. Skills Lab helps you build practical skills in fast-growing spaces like tech, green innovation, tourism and agriculture. As the world changes, so will opportunities, and we're committed to building pathways that are market-aligned.

In Umuzi's Skills Lab, you'll find a variety of learning programmes tailored to different needs:

  • Accredited programmes
  • Short skills courses and certifications
  • Other industry-aligned pathways

Every programme is designed to equip you with real-world, job-ready skills.

SYS.MOD // 02

Experience Lab: gain real work experience

We help you build real, meaningful experience through hands-on projects and freelance gigs that count. Whether you've just finished a qualification or have some training but no way to apply it - this Lab is built to bridge that gap.

Here's what to expect:

  • // Real-world projects tailored to your skills
  • // Impact gigs: small but powerful freelance opportunities that help you earn while learning
  • // Personalised experience pathways powered by smart data tools that connect you to the right projects at the right time

Your work in the Experience Lab allows you to showcase your skills and enrich your CV, which we use to advocate for you with our network of partners.

Learn More
SYS.MOD // 03

Launch Lab: take off into the world of work

The Launch Lab prepares you for the professional world and connects you with employers and partners who are looking for talent like yours. You'll get:

  • // Support from our Launch Lab team
  • // Help with preparing for interviews, updating CVs, and understanding the working world
  • // Access to job openings and earning opportunities
  • // Mentorship to help you thrive in your first role

This isn't just about getting any job. It's about helping you find a career where you can grow.
